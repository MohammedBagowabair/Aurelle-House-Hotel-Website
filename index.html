Welcome

A city hotel that feels like a private residence.

Aurelle House is a contemporary hotel concept for travellers who want the energy of Singapore outside and genuine calm inside. Warm materials, generous light and a small, attentive team shape every stay, whether you are here for three meetings or three slow days.

  • Rooms designed around sleep and quiet
  • Personal replies on WhatsApp, not a booking engine
  • Thoughtful service for business and leisure stays

FAQ

Good to know

How do I book a room?

Message us on WhatsApp with your dates and number of guests. We reply with available room types and current rates, then confirm your booking in writing.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 3:00 pm and check-out is until 12:00 pm. Early arrival or late departure can often be arranged; just ask when you enquire.

Can you arrange an airport transfer?

Yes. Private car transfers can be arranged on request. Share your flight details on WhatsApp and we will confirm the rate in advance.

Are children welcome?

Absolutely. Our Family Twin Room and Executive Suite suit families, and cots can be provided on request.

Is breakfast included?

Rates are offered both with and without breakfast. We will explain the options clearly before you confirm.

Is the hotel accessible?

Please tell us about any access needs when you enquire so we can recommend the most suitable room and arrange assistance.

Plan your stay with a person, not a form.

Send your dates on WhatsApp and we will reply with rooms, rates and availability.
